# Design System: EcoSphere Platform (Premium Monochrome Edition)

## Principles
1. **Monochrome Titanium Foundation:** Content sits on obsidian `#09090B` and dark titanium `#121215` cards with hairline `#27272A` borders. Zero green color wash or tint.
2. **High-Contrast Actions:** Primary actions are crisp solid white (`#FFFFFF`) with dark `#09090B` text. Secondary actions use recessed zinc controls (`#18181B`).
3. **Data Clarity:** Colored hues are strictly restricted to semantic telemetry status indicators (Green = Safe, Amber = Warning, Red = Critical, Sky Blue = Water).
4. **No Clutter & No Gimmicks:** Every element on screen must serve an operational purpose. Gimmicky simulated sliders, duplicate navigation buttons, and decorative badges are removed.
5. **Craft Floor:** 11px functional floor, Outfit for copy, JetBrains Mono for telemetry, WCAG AA contrast on every screen.
