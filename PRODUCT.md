# Product Context: EcoSphere — Environmental Telemetry & Intelligence Platform

## Purpose
A modern, high-precision environmental telemetry, sensor monitoring, and sustainability operations platform. It aggregates live air quality (AQI) and water quality (WQI) sensors, runs machine-learning predictive forecasting and anomaly detection, optimizes municipal waste routing, and tracks environmental incidents.

## Platform
Responsive Web Application (Data-dense desktop operations view + adaptable mobile layout).

## Stack
- Frontend: React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Leaflet / React-Leaflet, Chart.js, Lucide React
- Backend: FastAPI (Python 3.12), SQLAlchemy, SQLite Telemetry Database
- Design Standard: Impeccable + Anti-AI-UI (Linear/Vercel/Tremor calm, data-dense craft; zero AI slop, zero government branding, zero faux glassmorphism)

## Core Capabilities
1. **Interactive Station Radar:** Live geographical map plotting environmental telemetry nodes with instant parameter inspection (PM2.5, PM10, AQI, Dissolved Oxygen, pH, Turbidity).
2. **Predictive Analytics & Anomaly Detection:** 7-day ARIMA/ML trend forecasting and Isolation Forest anomaly screening for rapid incident prevention.
3. **Logistics & Eco-Routing Optimization:** TSP/VRP heuristic vehicle routing that minimizes fuel burn and carbon emissions.
4. **Incident Reporting & Triage:** Structured community and field report intake with severity triage, tracking IDs, and resolution workflows.
5. **Telemetry Ingestion Gateways:** Simulated streaming sensors and gateway sync.

## Anti-Slop Design Commitments
- **No Government Branding:** Strictly a clean, independent modern environmental intelligence product. No emblems, no ministry text, no bureaucratic forms.
- **Calm, High-Craft Aesthetic:** Deep obsidian and titanium surfaces (`#09090B`, `#121215`, border `#27272A`) with crisp white primary actions and monochrome typography. Border-only cards, purposeful radii (`rounded-md` on controls, `rounded-lg` on panels). Semantic colors (green, amber, red, sky) used exclusively for real-time sensor status.
- **Tabular Data:** Every number, timestamp, coordinate, and metric uses `tabular-nums font-mono`.
- **Zero Generic Hero:** No auto-rotating stock image carousels, no centered dual-CTA marketing heroes, no gradient-text hype copy.
